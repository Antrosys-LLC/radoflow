## 2. About Us

### Engineering Excellence in Textile Wet Processing
Operating from the Quaid-e-Azam Industrial Estate (Kot Lakhpat), Rado Dyeing & Printing Mills (Pvt) Ltd. is an established fabric wet-processing facility built on precision chemistry, modern mechanical engineering, and tight turnaround cycles.

As a registered member of the All Pakistan Textile Processing Mills Association (APTPMA), we partner with spinning units, knitters, garment manufacturers, and institutional brands. From bulk cotton jersey processing to complex synthetic and poly-cotton blends, our dye-house infrastructure bridges raw knitted/woven yardage with retail-ready textile standards.

### Core Strategic Advantages:
* **Central Industrial Hub:** Strategic access within Lahore's industrial corridor simplifies logistics and dispatch schedules for regional cutoff deadlines.
* **Process Automation:** Automated chemical dosing and temperature-controlled dye cycles limit thermal and mechanical shock on delicate fiber structures.
* **Industrial Stewardship:** Adherence to strict regulatory standards, resource-efficient water consumption cycles, and wastewater treatment protocols.

## 3. Core Capabilities & Services

### 3.1 Fabric Dyeing & Wet Processing
* **Substrates Handled:** 100% Cotton, Cotton/Lycra, Polyester/Cotton (PC), CVC blends, and synthetic knits.
* **Process Infrastructure:** High-temperature (HT) and atmospheric exhaust soft-flow dyeing vessels designed for low liquor ratios and zero abrasion marks.
* **Colorfastness & Uniformity:** Optimized dye formulas ensure deep shade penetration and zero side-to-center shading variation across massive yardage lots.

### 3.2 Preparatory Bleaching & Scouring
* Continuous and semi-continuous optical whitening, desizing, and enzymatic bio-polishing.
* Produces ultra-clean fabric grounds free of seed coats, waxes, and pectin for uniform subsequent dye penetration and bright white bases.

### 3.3 Textile Printing
* Precision pigment and reactive printing capabilities tailored for knitwear and casual lifestyle apparel.
* Sharp screen definition, excellent outline clarity, and superior tonal contrast without compromising fabric hand-feel.

### 3.4 Chemical & Mechanical Finishing
* **Finishes Offered:** Softeners (Silicone, Cationic), Anti-pilling, Moisture-wicking, Anti-microbial, and Water-repellent finishes.
* **Stenter Processing:** Width fixation, dimensional stabilization, and skew control ensuring fabric weight (GSM) and shrinkage meet rigorous buying house benchmarks.

## 4. Quality Control & Technical Lab Standards

Every yard processed at Rado undergoes systematic quality verification supported by our in-house technical laboratory.

### Key Laboratory Procedures:
* **Spectrophotometer Color Matching:** Digital formulation software and automated micro-pipetting to yield rapid, accurate lab dips matched against Pantone or physical swatches.
* **Fastness Verification:** Rigorous verification for:
  * Wash Fastness (ISO 105-C06)
  * Rubbing / Crocking Fastness — Wet & Dry (ISO 105-X12)
  * Perspiration & Water Fastness (ISO 105-E01 / E04)
* **Dimensional Stability Testing:** Strict batch checks measuring residual shrinkage, torque/spirality, and GSM uniformity prior to lot clearance.
